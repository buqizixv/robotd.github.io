Skild AI Unleashes the First Universal Robot Brain

Published June 8, 2026

In June 2026, Skild AI released Skild Brain 1.0, a single AI model that can control any robot without retraining.

The Scale of the Bet

The model was trained on data from 10,000 robots across 200+ hardware platforms with 500 billion parameters. It was trained on 12,000 NVIDIA H200 GPUs over five months. The training dataset encompasses 2.8 billion robot trajectories from real robots.

How It Works

Skild Brain serves as a shared representation layer converting task descriptions into hardware-agnostic action tokens. It takes 15-30 seconds of calibration on new hardware. The architecture uses a transformer decoder with continuous action-token prediction, maintaining sub-millimeter precision.

In benchmarks, Skild Brain achieves 73 percent success on 500 novel tasks across 50 unseen robot platforms, vs 53 percent for robot-specific policies and 31 percent for GPT-4o-based controllers.

Data Flywheel

Skild charges per robot per month with a data-sharing commitment. Early customers include Amazon Robotics, Boston Dynamics, Unitree, and Zhiyuan. The Amazon deal covers 15,000 warehouse robots, the largest robot foundation model deployment in history.

Industry Impact

A universal robot brain eliminates the need for custom control software for each platform. The value in robotics shifts from software to data. Skild Brain 2.0 with 1.2 trillion parameters is already in training.

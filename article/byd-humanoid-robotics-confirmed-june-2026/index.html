BYD Confirms Humanoid Robotics Division, June 2026

Published June 5, 2026

BYD has officially confirmed the establishment of a dedicated humanoid robotics division as of June 2026, signaling the electric vehicle giant's strategic expansion into embodied artificial intelligence. The new division will leverage BYD's expertise in battery technology, manufacturing scale, and supply chain integration to develop humanoid robots for industrial and commercial applications.

This confirmation follows months of speculation in both the Chinese and international robotics communities. BYD's entry into the humanoid sector adds a major player to an increasingly competitive landscape that already includes Tesla's Optimus, Xiaomi's CyberOne, and numerous startups. The company's vertically integrated manufacturing capabilities and access to affordable battery cells could give it significant cost advantages in humanoid robot production.

Further details are expected to emerge in the coming weeks as BYD unveils its roadmap and initial hardware designs.
